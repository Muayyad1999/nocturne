# qos-synthetic-no-queues

A router with no queue in either menu.

**Both files are `origin: synthetic`: written by hand.** Each holds only the legend line that
RouterOS prints above a listing, copied from the documented samples (`qos-doc-queue-tree` for
the tree; the `/queue simple print` samples on MikroTik's page "DHCP" for the simple queues).
That a device with no queues prints the legend and nothing else is what the other empty
listings in this profile's recorded captures show; it was not recorded for these two commands.

Collected, this must be a table of zero rows — a read that worked and found nothing — and not
a failure.
